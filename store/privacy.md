## English

realistic minesweeper is an offline-playable Minesweeper game. Norns Interactive does not collect or upload personal information through the game. The game has no advertising, behavioral analytics, developer telemetry, accounts, chat or in-app purchases.

The device stores settings such as language, graphics quality, volume and accessibility options, together with completed-game records: mode, board size, mine count, time and completion date. These records are used only for local settings and the scoreboard and are not sent to Norns Interactive. An in-progress board is not saved as a completion record. There is no cloud synchronization. Uninstalling or clearing local app data may remove records; some desktop environments may retain the local WebView data folder.

If you enable device tilt, compatible-device orientation readings are used only for live lighting calculations. They are not persistently stored or uploaded. The game does not need access to contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

Microsoft Store, Windows and WebView2 may process installation, update, runtime or diagnostic information according to Microsoft's own settings and privacy statement. These are Microsoft platform services, separate from the game sending data to its developer. See https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement .

If you seek support through the GitHub Issues link above, your submissions are handled under GitHub's privacy practices. Public issues can be read by other people. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information. Changes to this policy will be published on this page. Contact the publisher through the same support channel for privacy questions.
